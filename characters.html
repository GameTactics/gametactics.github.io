⚔️ Sakiri (S+) – Offensive Support

  • Best Arcana Set: "Rogue" (2pc CRIT Rate + 4pc CDMG on ult) or "Chaos Weaver" for skill haste.
  • Main Stats: ATK% / CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG / Energy Recharge (sands if needed).
  • Skill Priority: Special Skill > Ultimate > Basic Attack.
  • Team Example: Sakiri + Nanally + Zero (Healer flex).
  • Rotation: Sakiri Skill (debuff) → swap to Nanally → trigger Esper Cycle → repeat.
Mindscape 1 (C1) greatly improves her debuff duration — high value.

Nanally (S+) – Off-field Main DPS

  • Best Arcana Set: "Scorching" (4pc – increases off-field damage by 45%).
  • Main Stats: ATK% / CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG (no need for ER, her summon deals consistent damage).
  • Skill Priority: Skill (summon) > Ultimate > Basic Attack.
  • Team Example: Nanally + Sakiri + any healer.
  • Playstyle: Summon Underboss, swap to main driver, let Nanally’s summon auto-attack while you control another character.

Chiz (S+) – Free S-rank DPS

  • How to obtain: Reach City Tycoon level 18 and complete her story commission.
  • Best Arcana Set: "Rhythm Breaker" (increases combo damage).
  • Main Stats: CRIT DMG > ATK% > CRIT Rate.
  • Skill Priority: Ultimate (grants rhythm stacks) > Skill > Basic Attack.
  • Mechanic: Tap skill on beat indicators to maximize damage. Practice in training mode.

S-Tier Sub-DPS: Jiuyuan & Baicang

Jiuyuan (Psyche): Build with "Echo" arcana set to enhance detonation damage. Best paired with Psyche/Lakshana teams.

Baicang (Incantation): Use "Spellblade" set. Rotation: Skill → Enhanced Attack → Ultimate. Prioritize CRIT Rate to 70%+.

F2P Builds: Esper Zero & Haniel

Esper Zero (MC): Very flexible. Build either "Rogue" for damage or "Defender" for survival. His unique Instant Cycle can skip 2 elements in reactions – useful for bosses.

Haniel: Best support for beginners. Equip "Symphony" arcana to boost team ATK on ultimate. Prioritize HP% and Energy Recharge.
