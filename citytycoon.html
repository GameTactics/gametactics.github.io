City Tycoon Complete Guide

Make Millions of Fons · Unlock Chiz · Buy Houses & Cars

✅ Updated May 2026 · Version 1.0

️ City Tycoon Overview

Unlocked after completing main story chapter 1 (mission "Good Morning Hethereau"). This is a side progression system that generates Fons, the primary currency for gear upgrades, character ascension, and housing. It also unlocks exclusive rewards including the S-rank character Chiz.

Fastest Leveling Roadmap

  • Level 1 → Start with daily commissions: deliver parcels and ride-hailing (unlocked automatically after story).
  • Level 2 → Buy your first car (used sedan, 5,000 Fons). Ride-hailing jobs now pay 2,000-5,000 Fons per trip.
  • Level 3 → Unlock delivery service (more EXP per commission).
  • Level 4 → Buy the coffee shop at Bluebeard Road (15,000 Fons). Passive income every 72 hours.
  • Level 5 → Buy your first apartment (30,000 Fons). Adds +500 Fons/day passive.
  • Level 10 → Unlock "Paw Clutch Heist" weekly event (up to 1,000,000 Fons per week).
  • Level 18 → Unlock Chiz (S-rank DPS) and her full awakening sequence.
Pro tip: Always complete all 3 daily commission tasks. Each gives 500 Tycoon EXP, which is the fastest way to level up.

Best Ways to Earn Fons (Prioritized)

  • Ride-hailing (Level 2+): Best early income. Accept long-distance trips for higher pay. Use sports car to reduce travel time.
  • Coffee Shop (Level 4): Restock every 3 days. Assign Espers to increase profit margin (Sakiri gives +15%).
  • Paw Clutch Heist (Level 10): Weekly stealth mission. Rewards scale with your management level – aim for perfect stealth for max Fons.
  • Apartment Rent (Level 5+): Passive daily income. Upgrade with furniture (buy from City Tycoon store) to increase rent.
  • Stock Market (Level 15): Advanced – requires initial capital. Buy low, sell high. Follow in-game market trends.

️ Cars & Porsche Collaboration (May 2026)

  • Starter car: Sedan (5,000 Fons) – available at level 2.
  • Sports car: Unlock at level 8 (80,000 Fons) – faster travel speed, higher ride-hailing payout (x1.5).
  • Porsche Taycan: Limited event car (May 2026). Obtain via event tokens or premium shop. Special effect: +20% Fons from ride-hailing.
  • Car customizations: Paint jobs and decals available at level 12 – purely cosmetic but increase daily commission EXP.

Unlocking Chiz – Full Guide

Reach City Tycoon level 18 and complete the story commission "Chiz's Dream." After that, you can recruit her for free. Her mindscapes (awakenings) are unlocked by continuing to level Tycoon to 25 (C1), 30 (C2), and 35 (C3). Chiz is a top-tier DPS with rhythm-based combat – well worth rushing Tycoon levels.

⭐ Chiz tip: Her damage scales with perfect timing on skill activations. Practice in the training room to master the beat indicator.
