Esper Cycle System – Combat Deep Dive

Master the 6-element reaction chain for massive damage

✅ Updated May 2026 · Version 1.0

What is the Esper Cycle?

The Esper Cycle is Neverness to Everness's core combat mechanic. Six elements (Lakshana → Cosmos → Anima → Incantation → Chaos → Psyche → back to Lakshana) form a fixed reaction order. Triggering adjacent elements creates powerful reactions; triple combos unlock Charged or Discord states.

Triple Cycle: Charged & Discord

When you trigger three consecutive reactions in order, you enter a special state:

  • Charged (Top Trio): Lakshana → Cosmos → Anima. All party members gain 20% Ultimate energy and 30% ATK for 12s.
  • Discord (Bottom Trio): Incantation → Chaos → Psyche. Deals massive Break damage (stagger) to all enemies, ignoring 50% of their Break resistance.
⚔️ Combo Tip: In boss fights, aim for Discord to break boss quickly, then use Charged to burst down during stagger.

Team Building Strategies

Build your team around the element chain. Two common archetypes:

  • Top Trio Team: Lakshana (Haniel) + Cosmos (Zero/Chiz) + Anima (Nanally). Excellent for sustained damage and ultimate spam.
  • Bottom Trio Team: Incantation (Baicang) + Chaos (Sakiri) + Psyche (Jiuyuan). Best for burst damage and breaking tough enemies.
  • Hybrid Team: Use four elements to trigger both Charged and Discord in one rotation, but requires precise swapping.

Recommended starter team: Sakiri (Chaos) + Nanally (Anima) + Zero (Cosmos) — covers three elements, easy to execute.

⏱️ Combat Rotation Example (Bottom Trio)

  • Step 1: Open with Incantation character (Baicang) – apply Incantation with skill.
  • Step 2: Swap to Chaos character (Sakiri) – use skill to trigger Scorch (Incantation → Chaos).
  • Step 3: Immediately swap to Psyche character (Jiuyuan) – use ultimate or enhanced attack to trigger Nova (Chaos → Psyche).
  • Result: Discord activates, dealing massive Break damage and stunning the boss.
  • Step 4: While boss is staggered, unload all ultimates and heavy attacks.
️ Practice: The training room in the garage allows you to set enemy dummy to test rotations. Use it to perfect your timing.

Break & Ultimate Energy Mechanics

  • Break Bar: Appears under boss HP. Fills when you use reactions (especially Discord). Once full, boss is stunned for 8s and takes +50% damage.
  • Ultimate Energy: Generated by normal attacks, skills, and Charged state. Save ultimates for break windows.
  • Swap Cooldown: 2 seconds between swaps. Plan your rotation to avoid downtime.
