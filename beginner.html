Reroll Guide (Get the Best Start)

Target Sakiri (S+) or Nanally (S). Follow these steps for efficient reroll:

  • Step 1: Use guest login or a new email account. Each server counts separately — 4 servers per account means 4 free rerolls.
  • Step 2: Play through tutorial until you defeat the first boss (Picture Frame Anomaly). Takes ~6-8 minutes.
  • Step 3: Claim all pre-registration and launch rewards from in-game mail: 120+ pulls, S-rank selector, 1600 Annulith.
  • Step 4: Pull on the starter banner (guaranteed S-rank within 50 pulls).
  • Step 5: If you didn't get Sakiri or Nanally, go to Settings → Account → Delete Account and repeat.
Pro tip: The S-rank selector from launch rewards can be used to pick Sakiri if you get another S-rank from the banner.

⭐ Best Starter Characters (Free & Easy)

  • Sakiri (S+) – Best offensive support. Use S-rank selector on her if possible.
  • Nanally (S) – Top tier DPS, great for beginners.
  • Zero (MC) – Versatile, free, useful throughout the game.
  • Haniel (A) – Free from story, decent healer.

Recommended starter team: Sakiri + Nanally + Zero. This team covers three elements for reactions and has strong synergy.

First 7 Days Roadmap

  • Day 1: Complete chapter 1 → unlock City Tycoon. Spend stamina on anomaly suppression. Reach character level 15.
  • Day 2: Reach City Tycoon level 2 → buy first car. Start ride-hailing for Fons. Clear daily commissions.
  • Day 3: Level main character to 20 → unlock first arcana set. Farm materials for ascension.
  • Day 4: Upgrade City Tycoon to level 4 → buy coffee shop. Passive income starts.
  • Day 5: Push main story to chapter 3. Unlock anomaly commissions.
  • Day 6: Clear first Chaos stage (Difficulty I). Claim milestone rewards.
  • Day 7: Buy first apartment (Tycoon level 5). You now have stable Fons income and can tackle harder content.

✅ Daily Checklist (10-15 min)

  • ✔️ Claim login rewards & check redeem codes (see Updates page).
  • ✔️ Spend 160 stamina on anomaly suppression or material domains. Priority: character ascension mats > arcana exp > weapon mats.
  • ✔️ Complete 3 City Tycoon daily tasks (ride-hailing, delivery, coffee shop).
  • ✔️ Do 1 anomaly commission (purple or gold if available).
  • ✔️ Upgrade one character's talent or level.
  • ✔️ Check Hunter's Guide for free rewards.

F2P Tips & Resource Management

  • Annulith (pull currency): Never spend on standard banner. Save for limited characters. You can earn ~90 pulls per month as F2P.
  • Stamina (Character Pixels): Refreshes at 1 per 6 min. Don't waste on low-level domains. Save for higher tier drops.
  • Arcana (gear): Don't level blue arcana. Wait for purple/gold. Main stat > set bonus early.
  • Fons (gold): Use for character ascension and Tycoon investments first. Avoid buying low-tier items from shops.
⚠️ Common mistakes: Don't spread resources across too many characters. Focus on your main team of 3-4. Don't ignore City Tycoon – it's your main Fons income.

❓ Beginner FAQ

  • Q: Is the game pay-to-win? A: No. No 50/50 gacha. F2P can clear all content with proper team building.
  • Q: Which server should I choose? A: Pick the server closest to your region for low ping. Accounts are region-locked.
  • Q: How to get more pulls? A: Main story, anomaly commissions, Hunter pass, events, and code redemption.
  • Q: Should I level all characters? A: No. Focus on 3-4 main characters. Resources are scarce early.
  • Q: When is the best time to spend stamina? A: After reaching character level 30, when higher tier domains unlock.
