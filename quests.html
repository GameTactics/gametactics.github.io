Main Story Overview

The main story is divided into chapters. Completing them unlocks new areas, City Tycoon features, and character recruitment. Below is a chapter-by-chapter guide.

Chapter 1: "Unforeseen Yet Foretold" (Prologue)

  • Objective: Complete tutorial, meet Mint, and clear the first anomaly.
  • Key steps: Exit garage → talk to Nelly → meet Mint at director's office → enter anomaly dimension → find Mint → fight two small monsters → learn Esper Cycle → critical dodge tutorial → defeat Picture Frame Anomaly boss.
  • Boss tip: The Picture Frame attacks with projectiles and area slams. Dodge red indicators, use critical dodges to trigger slow-mo counterattacks. Build Break meter with Chaos element.
  • Rewards: 200 Annulith, Character EXP materials, unlock City Tycoon.
Pro tip: After the boss, you'll receive a free 10-pull ticket. Use it on starter banner.

Chapter 2: "Welcome to Hethereau"

  • Unlock: After reaching City Tycoon level 2.
  • Objectives: Explore the city, complete three anomaly commissions, and meet key NPCs (coffee shop owner, car dealer).
  • Hidden side quest: Talk to the street musician near the fountain – play along with his rhythm mini-game for 50 Annulith.
  • Rewards: 300 Annulith, 20k Fons, unlock ride-hailing.

Chapter 3: "Chaos Under the Neon"

  • Requirements: City Tycoon level 4, character level 20+.
  • Main boss: Neon Corruptor (Incantation element).
  • Strategy: Use Chaos element (Sakiri) to trigger Scorch reaction. Boss summons electric fields – move away from glowing floor tiles. After 50% HP, boss gains shield; break it with 2 Discord rotations.
  • Rewards: 500 Annulith, S-rank arcana box (choose "Rogue" or "Scorching").

Anomaly Commissions (Daily / Weekly)

After chapter 2, you unlock anomaly commissions – repeatable missions that reward Hunter EXP, upgrade materials, and Annulith.

  • Green (Easy): Defeat 10 basic enemies. Reward: 10 Annulith, 1 blue mat.
  • Blue (Medium): Clear anomaly zone with 2 mini-bosses. Reward: 30 Annulith, 2 purple mats.
  • Purple (Hard): Complete within time limit (5 min). Reward: 60 Annulith, 1 gold mat, chance of arcana drop.
  • Gold (Weekly): Defeat a named boss. Reward: 150 Annulith, 3 gold mats, exclusive title.
Efficiency tip: Always pick purple/gold commissions. They give the best rewards for stamina spent.

Hidden Side Quests & Collectibles

  • Lost Cat in Bluebeard Road: Find the cat near the dumpster, follow it to owner – reward: 20k Fons.
  • Graffiti Artist: Spray 5 hidden graffiti spots around the city – reward: exclusive car decal.
  • Time Capsule: Behind the coffee shop, dig with shovel (buy from general store) – reward: 100 Annulith.
  • Memory Orbs: 50 orbs scattered across the map. Collect 10/20/30/40/50 for Annulith and ascension materials.
