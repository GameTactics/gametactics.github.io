Patch Notes – Version 1.0.2 (May 21, 2026)

  • Balance changes: Sakiri's debuff duration reduced from 8s to 6s. Nanally's off-field damage increased by 5%.

  • New content: Hotori's story quest added. New City Tycoon decorations.
